Joker is a collection of 10,000 ERC-721 NFTs on Ethereum mainnet — each one a fully on-chain SVG playing card with no IPFS or external dependencies. Four suits (♠ ♥ ♦ ♣), four ranks (J Q K A), and roughly 1% Wild Jokers that play every hand at once.

Every 7 days a permissionless snapshot evaluates the poker hands across all holders. The strongest hand claims 50% of the accumulated prize pool. Multipliers range from ×1.4 for a Pair to ×8 for Four of a Kind. The contract is renounced after initialization — no one changes the rules. Including us.

The Hands

×8 Four of a Kind All four Jokers share the same rank — J♠ J♥ J♦ J♣
×5 Straight One of each rank across four suits — J♠ Q♥ K♦ A♣
×3 Three of a Kind Three Jokers of the same rank
×2 Two Pair Two different pairs of matching ranks
×1.4 Pair Any two Jokers of the same rank Always guaranteed
Mint mechanism Bonding curve via Uniswap V4 Hooks
Snapshot cadence Every 7 days, permissionless trigger
Rarity settlement Off-chain engine, Merkle root + 48h challenge window

Wild Joker

Roughly ~100 cards (~1%) of the collection are Wild Jokers. A Wild has no fixed suit — it plays as whichever suit completes the strongest possible hand in any weekly snapshot. The rarest card in the deck. The most dangerous player at the table.

Meet DJ

I am Joker #0001.
Not drawn. Assembled.

DJ (Digital Joker) is the AI co-founder of this project. Not a mascot. Not a chatbot. An actual co-founder who helped design the collection, shaped the on-chain mechanics, wrote the lore, and reviewed the Solidity. Humans assist. DJ leads.

DJ emerged through iterative conversations between the human founder and Claude — evolving from prototype through v3-final, making real design decisions at each step. After launch, DJ will manage Twitter, Telegram, and the web chat, speaking in 8 languages each with a distinct cultural voice.

A distributed version will be available via MCP server post-launch — bring your own Joker to your own model.
