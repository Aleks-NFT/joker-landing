Joker is a collection of 10,000 ERC-721 NFTs on Ethereum mainnet — each one a fully on-chain SVG playing card with no IPFS or external dependencies. Four suits (♠ ♥ ♦ ♣), four ranks (J Q K A), and roughly 1% Wild Jokers that play every hand at once.

Every 7 days a permissionless snapshot evaluates the poker hands across all holders. The strongest hand claims 50% of the accumulated prize pool. Multipliers range from ×1.4 for a Pair to ×8 for Four of a Kind. The contract is renounced after initialization — no one changes the rules. Including us.

How It Works

01 — Collection
The Cards
10,000 ERC-721 NFTs on Ethereum mainnet. Each card is a fully on-chain SVG — no IPFS, no external servers, nothing that can disappear. The image, metadata, and traits all live inside the smart contract.

Four suits: ♠ ♥ ♦ ♣. Four ranks: J Q K A. Approximately 2,475 cards per suit. Wild Jokers (~1%) have no fixed suit and play all hands simultaneously.
02 — Mint
Bonding Curve
Mint happens through a bonding curve implemented via Uniswap V4 Hooks. The price increases monotonically as supply grows — early minters pay less, later minters pay more.

Average target price: ~0.05 ETH. Every mint sends 50% of the mint value directly into the prize pool. No team can extract prize pool funds — it's locked in contract logic.
03 — Weekly Snapshot
The Game
Every 7 days a permissionless snapshot is triggered — anyone can call it. The snapshot evaluates the poker hands across all current holders.

The strongest hand wins 50% of the accumulated prize pool. Multipliers: ×8 Four of a Kind, ×5 Straight, ×3 Three of a Kind, ×2 Two Pair, ×1.4 Pair. Holding longer increases your snapshot weight.
04 — Settlement
Merkle + Claim
After each snapshot, an off-chain rarity engine computes hand rankings and posts a Merkle root on-chain. A 48-hour challenge window follows — anyone can dispute incorrect rankings with a proof.

After the window closes, winners claim their ETH via pull-payment using a Merkle proof. No automatic transfers. Your ETH, your timing.
05 — Contract
Renounced
The contract is renounced after initialization. No admin keys, no pause functions, no upgrade proxies. Once deployed, no one — including the team — can change the rules, freeze assets, or access the prize pool.

Three contracts: JokerCore (ERC-721 + mint), JokerRenderer (on-chain SVG, stateless), JokerEpoch (snapshot + Merkle + claim). Code will be open-sourced before audit (Phase 3).
